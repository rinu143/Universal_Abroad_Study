Georgia




Georgia is a country located in the Caucasus region of Eurasia, at the crossroads of Europe and Asia. It is a beautiful country with a rich history and culture. Georgia is becoming an increasingly popular destination for international students, due to its affordable tuition fees, high-quality education, and safe and welcoming environment.
